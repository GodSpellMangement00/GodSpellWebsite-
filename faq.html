What is GodSpell?

GodSpell is a Minecraft Survival clan built as a family, not just a grinding team.

Which game does GodSpell play?

We focus only on Minecraft Survival.

Which server does GodSpell play on?

We play on the Pika Network server: pika-network.net.

How can I join GodSpell?

Join our Discord server and apply for Trial member.

What is a Trial Member?

A Trial member is someone being tested for activity, behavior, and loyalty.

Can Trial members become Core members?

Yes. Trial members can become Core members after proving themselves.

Can Trial members become staff?

Yes. Trial members can become staff even before becoming Core members.

What is a Core Member?

Core members are trusted, loyal players who represent GodSpell.

Is there any age limit?

No age limit. We expect maturity and respectful behavior.

Which language should I speak?

English only, for clear communication.

Are toxic players allowed?

No. Toxicity, hate, racism, or harassment is strictly not allowed.

Do you host giveaways?

Yes. We host giveaways and fun community events.

Do you do PvP or only Survival?

We mainly focus on Survival grinding and building.

Can I join if I am new to Minecraft?

Yes. Beginners are welcome if they are willing to learn.

Is GodSpell a competitive clan?

We are competitive in grinding but friendly in community.

Can I invite my friends?

Yes, as long as they follow the rules and respect others.

Who are the leaders of GodSpell?

The leaders are Sam and Bronzey.

When was GodSpell established?

GodSpell was established in 2024.

What is GodSpell’s motto?

Not just to grind — we build a family and connect people worldwide.
