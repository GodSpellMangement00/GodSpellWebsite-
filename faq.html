What is GodSpell?

GodSpell is a Minecraft Survival clan built as a calm, friendly family.

Which server do you play on?

We play on pika-network.net in Survival mode.

Who are the leaders?

The leaders of GodSpell are Sam and Bronzey.

Is there any age limit?

No age limit. We only expect maturity and respect.

Is toxicity allowed?

No. Toxicity, hate, or disrespect is strictly not allowed.

How can I join GodSpell?

Join our Discord server and contact the staff.

How can I become a Core Member?

Be active, respectful, and apply via Discord.

Do you only play Survival?

Yes, GodSpell focuses only on Minecraft Survival.
